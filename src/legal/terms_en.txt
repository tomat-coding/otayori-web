(4) Prices, periods and free trials are shown on the subscription screen.

Each photo is at most 3 MB and a JPEG, and uploads per hour and per day may be limited when use goes far beyond normal, such as automated bulk uploads. Sharing your family’s handouts in the usual way never reaches these limits.
