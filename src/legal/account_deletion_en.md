title: "Otayorin: deleting your account and data"
---

How to delete your Otayorin account (operated by Tomasz Matusik) and the data kept on our servers.

## In the app

1. Open "Account" on the Family tab and choose "Delete account".
   Or choose "Delete account and all data" under Family → Settings.
2. Confirm. The account is deleted at once and can't be restored.

## Without the app

If you have deleted the app or don't have the device, email us:

- To: tomat.firma@gmail.com
- Subject: Account deletion request
- Include how you signed in (Apple or Google) and that account's email address.

We confirm that you are the account holder, delete the account and tell you by email when it's done. There is no fee.

## What is deleted

- Your account information (email address, user ID, display name)
- Your notification identifier and purchase records
- The record of who wrote or ticked the items that stay with your family
- If you used the app on your own: your family's schedule, children's information and shared photos

## What stays

- If your family has other members, the items and handouts you shared stay for them (without the record of who wrote
  them).
- Data on your devices stays until you use "Delete all data" in the app's Settings or delete the app.
- Information in server backups and access records disappears automatically after a period of time. Information the
  law requires us to keep is kept for that period.

See "12. How long information is kept, and deleting it" in the privacy policy.
